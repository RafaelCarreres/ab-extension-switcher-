# Privacy Policy — A/B Extension Switcher

Last updated: August 2026

A/B Extension Switcher ("the extension") is committed to protecting your privacy.

## Data collection

The extension does **not** collect, store, transmit, or share any personal
data. It does not track your browsing activity, does not use analytics, and
does not send any information to external servers.

## Local storage

The extension stores only your configuration locally on your device using the
browser's `storage` API. This includes which extensions you have assigned to
Side A and Side B, and which side is currently active. This data never leaves
your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

## Permissions

The extension uses the `management` permission solely to enable and disable the
extensions you have assigned to each group when you switch between Side A and
Side B. It does not read, modify, or transmit information about your other
extensions for any other purpose.

## Third parties

No data is sold, transferred, or shared with any third party.
